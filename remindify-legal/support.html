FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases", or open the Premium screen and tap "Restore".

How do I enable iCloud sync?

Go to Settings and toggle iCloud sync on. Make sure you are signed into iCloud on your device.

My notifications are not working

Make sure notifications are enabled for Remindify in your device Settings > Notifications > Remindify.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all data. If iCloud sync is enabled, you can also remove data from Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage.
