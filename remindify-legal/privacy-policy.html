Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Overview

Remindify is a reminder and task management app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data Collection

Remindify stores your reminders, categories, and preferences locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your personal iCloud account managed by Apple.

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. We do not use analytics or tracking tools.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your reminder data is synced across your devices using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your private iCloud container and is not accessible to us.

Notifications

Remindify uses local notifications to alert you about your reminders. Notification data stays on your device and is not sent to any server.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
