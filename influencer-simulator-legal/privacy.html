Privacy Policy Last updated: June 22, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how Be an Influencer Simulator ("the App", "we") handles information. 1. Data we collect None. Be an Influencer Simulator is a fully offline, single-player game. The App does not collect, store on our servers, share, sell, or transmit any personal data. 2. Data stored on your device The App stores your game progress (player name, posts, followers, in-game balance, settings) locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This information never leaves your device, is not synced to any cloud service, and is not accessible to us. You can erase all locally stored data at any time by opening Settings in the App and tapping Reset Game, or by uninstalling the App. 3. Third-party services The App does not embed any analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDK that collects data. There are no trackers. 4. In-app purchases If the App offers optional premium features in the future, payment processing will be handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not see or store your payment information; we only receive an anonymous purchase receipt verifying the purchase. 5. Children's privacy The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone of any age, since it collects no information at all. 6. Changes to this policy If we make material changes to this policy, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be announced inside the App. 7.
